Site24x7 starts with a very affordable plan, which makes it an easy option to consider when you are comparing monitoring tools.

But the starting price is only part of the story.

Site24x7 uses a base plan with add-ons, so your costs can go up as you add more monitors, log volume, synthetic checks, RUM pageviews, or network components.

So, it is worth looking at the full pricing structure before deciding if Site24x7 fits your budget.

In this guide, we break down the three Site24x7 plans, cover the main add-ons, and explain how the cost can change as your monitoring needs grow.

What Is Site24x7?

Site24x7 is a cloud monitoring tool from Zoho, sold under the ManageEngine brand. It watches websites, servers, networks, applications, and cloud services from one place.

It brings a lot of monitoring into one tool. You get website and uptime checks, server and infrastructure monitoring, network monitoring, application performance monitoring, log management, and real user monitoring, all in the same place.

The main draw is the low starting price and the wide coverage. A small team can start cheap and add more monitoring as it grows, which is why so many smaller businesses pick it.

The thing to keep in mind is how the pricing works. Because Site24x7 charges a base plan plus add-ons, your total cost depends on how much you monitor, not just the plan you start on.

How Site24x7 Pricing Works

Site24x7 pricing comes down to two things: the plan you pick, and the add-ons you buy on top.

The plan sets your base. Each one gives you a fixed number of monitors, websites, servers, and other limits, so you pick the one that fits your size.

The add-ons cover everything beyond that. Once you go past the plan limits, you buy more in blocks, like extra monitors, more log volume, or more RUM pageviews.

Then there is billing. You can pay monthly or annually, and paying annually is cheaper, so a yearly plan costs less per month.

The Add-On Model

The add-ons are what make Site24x7 hard to predict, so they are worth understanding early.

You can add basic monitors, host monitors, advanced monitors, network components, RUM pageviews, logs, and synthetic runs. Each one is sold in blocks, and the rate changes between the Lite, Professional, and Enterprise plans.

Logs are the add-on to watch most. The price rises with both the volume you send and how long you keep it indexed, so a large log setup can cost more than the plan itself.

What Increases Your Site24x7 Costs

Five things lift a Site24x7 bill above the plan price.

  1. Log volume and index period: Logs are priced by how much you send and how long you keep them indexed, so a large or long-kept log setup can cost more than the plan itself.

  2. Monitor count: More basic, host, and advanced monitors all add to the bill, since each block is a separate add-on.

  3. Synthetic runs: Synthetic checks are billed by the number of runs, so frequent checks across many locations add up fast.

  4. RUM pageviews: Real user monitoring is priced by pageviews, so a busy site uses more and lifts the total on its own.

  5. Network components: Each network component you monitor is billed in blocks, so a large network raises the cost as you add devices.

In short, your Site24x7 cost depends on how many add-ons you stack on your plan. It works best for teams that watch their log volume and monitor count closely.

Site24x7 User Reviews: What Teams Actually Say

This section is based on our analysis of Site24x7 reviews across G2, Capterra, and similar platforms. Site24x7 reviews well on price and coverage, and less well on cost clarity as you grow.

The praise is consistent. Most reviewers point to the low price and wide coverage first, and like getting website, server, network, and cloud monitoring in one tool.

Easy setup comes up a lot too. Reviewers say it is quick to get going, with reliable alerts and clear dashboards once it is running.

People also like the flexible add-on model. Teams can start small and add only the monitoring they need.

The main complaints are about cost and complexity as you scale. Reviewers say the pricing gets confusing with many add-ons, and that the interface can feel cluttered.

The learning curve and billing come up too. Some reviewers say advanced setup takes time, and that the bill grows and gets harder to track as the setup expands.

These are common review patterns, not hard facts, so your own results will depend on your monitor count and log volume.

FAQs

How Much Does Site24x7 Cost?

Site24x7 has three main plans. Lite is $10 per month, or $9 paid annually. Professional is $49 per month, or $42 paid annually. Enterprise starts at $625 per month paid annually. There is also an MSP plan at $59 per month, or $54 paid annually, and a free forever plan for up to 50 resources.


What Does the Site24x7 Professional Plan Include?

The Professional plan includes 1 application, 5 servers, 20 websites, 4 GB of log ingestion, 10 network components, 100K RUM pageviews, third-party integrations, and support for multiple users. Extra usage is billed as add-ons.


How Much Do Site24x7 Logs Cost?

On the Professional add-on rates, 1 TB of logs costs $195 for 7-day indexing, $450 for 15-day, and $900 for 30-day. Logs are the biggest cost driver, since the price rises with both volume and index period.


How Does Site24x7 RUM Pricing Work?

Site24x7 real user monitoring is priced by pageviews. On the Professional plan, 500K pageviews cost $25 and 10M pageviews cost $300. You can buy more pageview blocks as add-ons.


Does Site24x7 Have a Free Plan?

Yes. Site24x7 has a free forever plan that covers uptime monitoring for up to 50 resources, with downtime email alerts. It suits a small personal site, but growing teams will need a paid plan.


What Are the Hidden Costs of Site24x7?

The main ones are log indexing costs, extra monitors, synthetic runs, RUM pageviews, and network components. All of these are add-ons billed on top of the plan, so the bill grows as your setup grows.


Is Site24x7 Priced per User or per Monitor?

Site24x7 is priced by plan and by add-on, not per user. The plan sets your base limits, and you buy add-on blocks for more monitors, logs, pageviews, and other usage.


How Does Motadata Compare to Site24x7?

Site24x7 charges a plan plus many add-ons. Motadata gives you one fixed price for your whole setup, covering network, infrastructure, and app monitoring together, with AI included, so it sits as a simpler, more predictable choice.
