# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Sales engineers, solutions architects, and technical account operators evaluating and dispatching automated Site24x7 pricing quotations from incoming customer email inquiries.

## Product Purpose
Quote24x7 is an autonomous pricing assistant that ingests complex monitoring requirements via email, tracks multi-turn client clarifications, calculates mathematically verified plan options from the active Site24x7 catalogue, and coordinates outbound proposals with complete auditability.

## Positioning
A deterministic, zero-hallucination sales engineer console that refuses to invent rates or extrapolate unverified terms, linking every single line item to explicit client statements and verified catalogue pricing.

## Operating Context
High-volume inbound email processing (Resend webhooks), multi-turn conversation triage, deterministic PDF rendering, live delivery tracking, and rapid human-in-the-loop inspection.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- High-velocity Inbox triage with live thread statuses (`received`, `clarifying`, `quoted`, `needs_review`, `completed`).
- Comprehensive multi-turn conversation timeline with message direction, RFC header tracking, and attachment inspection.
- Transparent AI sales-engineer decision drawer displaying prompt reasoning, model metadata, and fallback triggers.
- Verified persistent storage (Supabase + in-memory fail-safe) and fail-closed deduplication.
- Dark-mode first high-density layout.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Quote24x7
- Palette & Vibe: Modern High-Density Operations Console — dark-mode first, refined monospace accents, subtle border hierarchy, crisp badges, and Linear/Datadog-level scanability.
- Voice: Precise, authoritative, transparent, zero fluff.

## Product Principles
1. **Operational Truth First**: Surfaces mirror reality without optimistic rounding or artificial delays.
2. **Dense Scanability**: Key metrics (counts, rates, states) are parseable in under 2 seconds without deep nesting.
3. **Transparent Decision Provenance**: AI reasoning, rules triggers, and pricing formulas are inspectable on demand.
4. **Resilient Interaction**: Instant search, fluid status filtering, and keyboard-friendly review flows.
